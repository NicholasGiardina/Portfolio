Mapping

Regional Fire-Use Research Map

This map shows the communities and places associated with the northern Alberta fire-use research alongside rivers, lakes, terrain, present-day reserve boundaries, and Wood Buffalo National Park. Point symbols distinguish primary ethnographic source locations from places included for broader geographic context.

CRS: NAD83(CSRS) / Alberta 10-TM (Forest) (EPSG:3402).

Sources: Government of Alberta / Alberta Data Partnerships Ltd. (reserve boundaries); Parks Canada (Wood Buffalo National Park boundary); Natural Resources Canada, Canada3D (elevation and derived hillshade); © OpenStreetMap contributors (Alberta boundary, rivers, and lakes), ODbL. Source and contextual locations compiled by Matthew Walls primarily from project source literature. Cartography by Nicholas Giardina.

My role: I created this map in QGIS using location data compiled by Dr. Matthew Walls and existing boundary, waterway, and elevation datasets. I organized and styled the layers, adjusted labels and the legend, and prepared the print layout for PNG and PDF export.

Meander River Close-Up Map

This map provides a closer view of Meander River, one of the dataset’s primary ethnographic source locations. Satellite imagery shows the surrounding landscape alongside the reserve boundary and the courses of the Hay and Meander rivers.

CRS: NAD83(CSRS) / Alberta 10-TM (Forest) (EPSG:3402).

Sources: Government of Alberta / Alberta Data Partnerships Ltd. (reserve boundary); © OpenStreetMap contributors (rivers), ODbL. Imagery: Esri World Imagery, Vantor WorldView-3 (WV03), acquired 17 June 2025. Source and contextual locations compiled by Matthew Walls primarily from project source literature. Cartography by Nicholas Giardina.
